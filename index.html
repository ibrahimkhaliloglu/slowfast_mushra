How natural does this synthesised speech sound?

These sentences were generated from ultrasound videos of the tongue, without any recorded audio. You will compare several versions of each sentence with the original recording and rate how close each one sounds to it. This is a MUSHRA test (MUltiple Stimuli with Hidden Reference and Anchor, ITU-R BS.1534).

How it works

  • There are 10 trials, one sentence each, about 20 minutes in total.
  • In each trial, play the Reference (the original recording), then play every sample A–J and rate how close each one sounds to it, from 0 to 100: how natural and how clear it is.
  • One of the samples is the reference itself, hidden: find it and rate it 100.

Trial 1 of 10

Example: try each of these

  • Play the Reference.
  • Play a sample, then click another one while it plays: it carries on from the same moment.
  • Click a waveform lane to play that sound from that moment.
  • Drag across a lane over one word: that part repeats, on every sound you switch to.
  • Press ⇄ Swap (or X) to flip between the last two sounds you played.
  • Rate the samples, and the one that sounds just like the reference 100.
  • Press Sort by score to line the samples up by your ratings and compare neighbours.

Keys: R reference, A–D samples, X swap, Space pause, ←→ 1 s.
